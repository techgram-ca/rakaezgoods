About Us

Your Global Partner in Fresh Produce

RAKAEZ GOODS WHOLESALERS L.L.C is a Dubai-based importer and wholesaler specialising in fresh fruit and vegetables sourced from growers all over the world. We move produce by air shipment for speed and by container shipment for volume — keeping the cold chain intact every step of the way.

From our Business Bay base, we re-export to markets across the GCC and deliver direct bulk supply of food items to restaurants throughout the UAE. Whatever your scale, we bring reliability, freshness and worldwide reach to your table.

  • Worldwide sourcing network
  • Air & container logistics
  • GCC re-export expertise
  • Restaurant & HORECA supply
