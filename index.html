Who We Are

A Dubai House Built on Freshness & Trust

Headquartered in Business Bay, RAKAEZ GOODS WHOLESALERS L.L.C is a premium importer and wholesaler of fresh fruit and vegetables. We connect the world's best growing regions with the Gulf's most demanding kitchens and retailers.

Our business moves produce two ways — air shipment for speed and peak freshness, and container shipment for scale — alongside dry-goods shipments from around the world. From our Dubai base we re-export across the GCC and supply food items in bulk directly to restaurants throughout the UAE.

Facilities & Operations

Precision From Port to Palette

Every consignment moves through a controlled, purpose-built operation designed to protect quality from the moment it lands to the moment it reaches you.

  • Temperature-controlled receiving & cold storageDedicated chilled zones calibrated per commodity to lock in freshness.
  • Air & sea freight coordinationPriority airport clearance and full-container consolidation through Dubai.
  • Grading, sorting & repackingProduce inspected, graded and repacked to each client's specification.
